# Crops

## Crops (`scripts/world/crop.gd`, `crop_plot.gd`, `crop_spot.gd`, `scripts/data/crop_data.gd`)

The GDD's farming: crops grow AMMO, drink FUEL, and their seeds are ORDINARY OBJECTS — `CropData.seed_item` + `seed_count` name a claim on a normal item (`seed` alone shadows the RNG built-in), per the design vault's Crops doc ("grows from light ammo box"): each ammo crop grows from its own rounds (light 20 / medium 15 / heavy 5, a quarter stack each — Economy balance fodder, like `CropPlot.water_liters`), the junk-grown species (pizza plants from sauce cans…) arrive with Crop content. There is NO seed item type — `SeedData` and `data/items/seeds/` are DELETED. `CropData` (`data/crops/`) is the species — `grow_days`, `regrow_days`, `produce` + `produce_count`, `seed_item` + `seed_count` — with the GDD's three authored (light bush 3d/15-30, medium plant 6d then DAILY/8-15, heavy vines 5d/5-10). `Crop` judges each day at MIDNIGHT off `Signals.calendar_updated`, looping `while last_absolute_day < today` so a multi-day sleep jump gets one verdict PER day and the watering only covers the first: watered → grow a day, dry → stagger, two dry days in a row → dead (the GDD's rule; ripe plants die of thirst too). Produce is rolled AT ripening into `pending_produce`; ripe fruit HOLDS until picked (no growth stacks behind it), and `harvest(inventory)` banks `Inventory.add`'s leftover back onto the plant, so a full pack never destroys a harvest — only a CLEARED plant restarts the clock at `regrow_days`. `water()` is deliberately cost-free on the CROP; the spend lives on the PLOT. THE INTERACTION LAYER IS IN — `game.tscn`'s `World/HomeBase/Garden` is a six-spot `CropPlot` growing all three species, and the BEDROLL finally stands beside the locker (it existed only as a scene before; multi-day growth is unplayable without sleeping the nights through). `CropSpot` (`scenes/props/crop_spot.tscn`, a Marker3D SUBCLASS — the plot still discovers plain markers, which is what probes build) carries the authored soil discs (dry/wet visibility toggle) and ONE InteractiveArea. It used to carry two — plant-or-harvest on interact, water on interact_secondary — stacked on the same point, which is two dots and two keys for one thing to walk up to; A SPOT IS ONE POINT AND ONE OFFER, and THE HAND says which job the key does: a fuel can in the active quick slot (`CharacterInventory.held_item`, the item slot the bar is on — not merely packed) means the player came to POUR, so watering outranks the harvest until the crop has drunk, and putting the can away hands the point back to plant-or-harvest. The label always names the press ("Water (1L)" / "Harvest (18 Light Rounds)"). THE SPECIES IS THE PLAYER'S CHOICE NOW (2026-08-12): bare soil advertises a plain "Plant" and taking it opens the PACK with a one-cell planting slot beside it (`PlantingPanelUI`, see Inventory) rather than planting on the spot. The plot used to choose — `plantable_species()` took the first kind the pack could afford in AUTHORED ORDER, so a player carrying both light and medium rounds could never grow the medium plant; that function and `plant_from_pack` are DELETED rather than left unreachable, and `species` is now the plot's offer list for the panel to walk, not a ranking. Bare soil therefore advertises whether or not the player carries seeds — the menu is where you find out what the plot could grow, so gating it on already holding the rounds would hide the answer behind the question. Offers follow the pump's conditional-advertising pattern: recomputed on area entry (the area's own enter-refresh reads `enabled` too early), on `crop.changed`, on `Signals.inventory_changed` since the PACK gates planting, and on `Signals.quick_slots_updated` so drawing the can re-aims the offer without the player moving a step. Still pending from the design: a build-style ground preview of where the can will pour. The VERBS live on the plot and spend from `player_pack()` (valid-checked, not null-checked — a scene reload leaves a freed player registered for a frame): `plant(kind, spot)` takes the species it is HANDED and claims the spot BEFORE anything is withdrawn, so a refused plant never eats seeds — the panel spends only after `plant` returns non-null, which is the same order the deleted `plant_from_pack` used; `water` drinks `water_liters` (1L default) all-or-nothing from the can IN HAND (`watering_can()`), not from anything merely carried — and raises the pack's `changed` itself, because a can's fill is entry state the grid can't see; `harvest` banks through the existing overflow rule. `Inventory` grew the generic withdrawal pair for it — `count_of(item)`/`take(item, count)` over the new `ItemData.same_kind` (script+name identity; `stacks_with` is now same_kind plus stackability) — beside the calibre-specific `spend_ammo`. The vessel: `FuelCanData` (`data/items/tools/fuel_can.tres`: 10L jerry can, 1x2, army-crate loot, `has_own_state`) with `add_fuel`/`drain` that RETURN what actually moved, filled free at the pump's `CanArea`; model = KayKit Resource Bits `Fuel_A_Jerrycan` (red; staged in `_not_exported/resource_bits/`, extracted by `extract_resource_bits.gd`), icon rendered from its mesh. `CropPlot` is the persistent one: crops are RUNTIME children the save's authored-nodes-only rule can't see, so the plot serializes its garden as rows and `load_state` replants them — re-stamping each crop's `last_absolute_day` from the clock, which the save applied before the nodes. `plant()` refuses a living occupant, clears a dead one, and wires `crop.changed` → the spot's `refresh_offers`. Crop visuals are placeholder but STATEFUL, authored in `crop.tscn` on the `changed` hook: the sprout cone scales with growth, fruit spheres show ripe, a flopped brown cone marks dead (the light bush visibly re-grows between harvests — accepted rather than inventing a maturity flag the save rows never carried). Probes: `crop_probe` (rules, 14) + `garden_probe` (interaction layer + the game.tscn wiring assertions, 22 — including the can moving pack → hand → away and the offer following it; the seed-claim checks moved out with the plot's own choosing) + `planting_probe` (ui/, 15 — the panel over the real garden). Still pending: crops/seed items on the truck's counter (the truck only BUYS today — selling is its own design), real plant art.

HOLD interactions: `InputManager` fires `just_pressed` normally, but an offer created with `p_hold` runs every frame the key is down (and its audio starts once instead of retriggering). `InteractiveArea` exposes it as `hold`.
