# Fuel

The fuel of this world is not gasoline: it is a special coolant-fuel (name TBD) that runs cold. That one conceit carries three systems at once: the land drinks it in place of water (crops are fuel-watered), an open barrel of it vents a pocket of cold air that holds the 10-16h scorch off (dripping black stains while it works; refilled by jerry can, with a future upgrade plumbing station barrels straight from the pump), and the pump sells the same stuff to travelers — the delivery truck's wholesale liters are the settlement's water, coolant and cash crop in one.
